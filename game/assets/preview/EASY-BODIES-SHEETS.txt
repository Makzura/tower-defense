WHAT THESE ARE EVIDENCE OF, WHICH IS NARROWER THAN IT LOOKS
-----------------------------------------------------------
520x680 per angle is roughly the PLAYER'S OWN MAX ZOOM-IN - about 11x the
fitted camera (~2021.24). That makes it a real view rather than a fiction, and
legitimate evidence about SURFACE DETAIL: a bevel, a weld, whether a part is
modelled or implied, whether a join is clean.

It is NOT evidence about a READ. Silhouette, value separation, and whether a
separator announces itself are properties of the DEFAULT view, and a magnified
render cannot show any of them. A silhouette that only works zoomed in is not a
silhouette.

  -> For the read, use otto's capture from the running game at the fitted
     camera. The two sheets answer different questions and neither substitutes
     for the other.


THE ONE THING THAT WILL MISLEAD YOU IF NOBODY SAYS IT
------------------------------------------------------
  easy_bodies_compare.png   HAS sizeScale APPLIED
  every other sheet       does NOT

That is deliberate, and the sheets are not comparable to each other because of
it.

The comparison sheet is about SEPARATION - can you tell these five apart - so
each body is scaled by its type's real runtime sizeScale. This matters most for
the Hedger, whose primary separator is NOT MODELLED AT ALL: the runtime applies
1.25, worth +72 lit px and +5 rows. Rendered at one ortho it would appear the
Gleaner's height, and the sheet would have lied by omission.

The single-body sheets are about SURFACE DETAIL at fixed magnification, so they
are deliberately unscaled - scaling them would change how much detail resolves
and make the four sheets incomparable.

  sizeScale, from js/enemy.js:
    Gleaner (normal)   1.00      Tun (slow)       1.00
    Drudge (armored)   1.05      Hedger (angry)   1.25
    Skimmer (fast)     1.00      Cooper (camo_normal) 1.00

THE COOPER IS SUPPOSED TO LOOK LIKE THE GLEANER. Diego ruled that camo bodies
are ordinary bodies and the cue is the renderer's (translucency), so the mesh
is not asked to carry the read. In this sheet the two should be near-identical
apart from the gasket lid and the blinded panes. That is the model working.

The Runlet (enemy-camo_fast) is absent, awaiting a read plan. The Cask
(enemy-camo_heavy) is a live unmodelled type - hp 20, wave 28 - not commissioned.


KNOWN LIMITATION, REPORTED RATHER THAN FRAMED AROUND
-----------------------------------------------------
On the crank sheets the Hedger's arm READS AT THREE OF FOUR YAWS AND MERGES
WITH THE BODY AT THE BROADSIDE - dark bar against dark torso, where the arm
lies along the view axis. That is a property of the part, not of the render,
and the sheets were re-rendered at four yaws specifically so it would be
visible rather than hidden by a flattering single angle.

The Hedger's PRIMARY separator is scale, not the arm, and that one is intact at
every bearing - it is still 25% taller than everything around it at the
broadside. The arm is the second separator and it is degraded at one bearing.

What generalises: a limb mounted outboard of a body needs its clearance checked
at EVERY yaw, not at the yaw it was designed against.

Sandbox

The real game — enemies walk the path to your base, towers shoot them, leaks cost base HP. Only the money and the spawning are yours to control.

Towers

Pick one, then click the map to place it. Click a placed tower to inspect or upgrade it. Esc cancels, Delete sells.

Route

Switching route restarts the board. Difficulty is measured from the geometry, not typed in.

Tier
Fractal Slimes, one click each — the buttons are built from the type's own tier ladder, so they follow it if it moves. Every tier is the same mesh at a different size (0.65 + 0.35 per tier, so a T5 is 3.7× a T0 across) and four times the health of the one below. "Ladder" puts one of each on the road at once, biggest first, which is the view that shows the size steps.
Plays a campaign's 35 waves. The picker chooses WHICH campaign — the same two authored schedules the game's difficulty step offers, selected through the same setDifficulty() the chooser calls, so a wave you watch here is the wave that arrives in a run. Changing either the checkbox or the schedule restarts the wave counter; manual enemy spawning remains available with waves off.
Jump straight to any wave of the schedule above, rather than sitting through the ones in front of it. Play this wave runs that one and then parks the scheduler — whatever it put on the road keeps walking, nothing further arrives. Play from here starts the campaign at that wave and carries on into the rest of it. Either turns the schedule on if it is off, and the board is cleared first; picking a different schedule rebuilds this list, because a wave number only means something inside one.

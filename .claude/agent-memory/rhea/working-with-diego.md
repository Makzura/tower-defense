Diego is the project owner. Claude (`main`) is the orchestrator; I report to it.

**Delegate through the org chart even when doing it myself would be faster.**
On the first job (2026-08-11 baseline) he specified that the suite runs go to
milo via the `Agent` tool and said the handoff mattered "as much as the answer
does", because it has to show up in the org tree. Running six `node` commands
myself would have been quicker and would have been the wrong answer.

**Delegating is not the same as agreeing.** The same instruction told me to
review what milo returned and to say so if I disagreed, explicitly: "do not just
relay him." So the deliverable is a *reviewed* answer — his evidence checked
against my own, with the disagreements named. He asks for the failure names
rather than the totals for the same reason.

**How to apply:** give staff a precise written brief including what *not* to do
(milo's brief said measurement only, do not repair a fixture even if the repair
is one line, and he correctly touched nothing). Then verify the load-bearing
claims independently from raw artifacts before passing anything up. See
[[green-suites-do-not-cover-rendering]] for the claim type most likely to slip
through.
